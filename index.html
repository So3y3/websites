How long does it take? +

Usually two weeks from our first chat to a live site. If you need it faster, tell me and I will see what I can do.

What about hosting and my domain? +

Your site is hand coded and static, so it can be hosted for free on solid, fast platforms. The only thing you keep paying for is your domain, and that is normal for any website.

What if I need changes later? +

Small tweaks are free for the first month after handover. After that we agree a fair price for anything bigger. And because you own every file, you can take your site anywhere, anytime.

Can you help with the words and photos? +

Yes. We work through your content together and I will help you say what you actually want to say. No photos? I will find proper free stock images that fit your vibe.

Because it should be. Websites are not magic, and small businesses should not pay agency prices or rent a template forever. I keep the process small and personal, so the price stays small too.
